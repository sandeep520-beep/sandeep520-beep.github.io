Research & Interests

My academic direction

Experimental Characterization and Mathematical Modeling of Carangiform Locomotion in Nile Tilapia

My research focuses on extracting fish body midline and caudal-fin motion from swimming videos using computer vision techniques.

The objective is to quantify swimming parameters such as:

• Tail-beat frequency
• Lateral amplitude
• Body curvature
• Wavelength
• Phase propagation
• Wave speed

This work combines experimental observation, computer vision and mathematical modeling to understand fish swimming locomotion.
