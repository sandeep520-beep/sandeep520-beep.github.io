Research Work

Current academic research

M.Tech Research · IIT Kharagpur

Experimental Characterization and Mathematical Modeling of Carangiform Locomotion in Nile Tilapia

My research investigates fish swimming locomotion using experimental observations, video analysis, and mathematical modeling.

The project aims to develop a computer-vision-based workflow for reconstructing body midline and caudal-fin motion from swimming videos.

Research Focus
  • Fish body midline extraction and reconstruction
  • Tail-beat frequency and lateral amplitude estimation
  • Body curvature and wavelength analysis
  • Phase propagation and swimming wave speed
  • Computer vision-based kinematic characterization
  • Mathematical modeling of fish locomotion
